# ApplyFlow Desktop 2.3.5 — Closed jobs and a clearer analytics view

## What changed

**1. Closed Job**
- When Jobright reports a posting is closed, ApplyFlow can record it as **Closed Job**. Stages from Saved through Applied, and No Response, move automatically. Interview, offer, rejection, and withdrawn stay as they are until you change them.
- The job panel shows the closed notice, with **Mark as Closed Job** or **Save as Closed Job**, and **Go to this application** once it is recorded.
- “You already applied” also has **Go to this application**.

**2. Analytics**
- The dashboard is through the current time: applied, waiting on a reply, active pipeline, interviews, offers, reply rate, follow-ups, and closed jobs.
- Daily or weekly activity, an outcome funnel, companies with reply counts, and a needs-attention list (overdue follow-ups and applications still at Applied after 14 days).
- Opening a row selects that application and scrolls it into view.

**3. Version**
- Application version **2.3.5** (`package.json` + `src/version.ts`).

## Installation

Quit ApplyFlow, then run `ApplyFlow-Setup-2.3.5.exe` (or `ApplyFlow-2.3.5-portable.exe`).
