# ApplyFlow Desktop 2.3.1 — Job titles open after company research

## What changed

**1. Claude.ai Chat**
- After company research, ApplyFlow clears Claude.ai’s “leave site?” prompt, finishes renaming that chat, and then opens the job-titles chat. A cancelled leave is retried once.
- When both a companies reply and a titles reply are on the page, ApplyFlow reads the titles JSON.

**2. Built-in browser**
- The Dice and Jobright buttons open in the tab you are on, including a new web tab. On the Jobs tab they still open there.

**3. Version**
- Application version **2.3.1** (`package.json` + `src/version.ts`).

## Installation

Quit ApplyFlow, then run `ApplyFlow-Setup-2.3.1.exe` (or `ApplyFlow-2.3.1-portable.exe`).
