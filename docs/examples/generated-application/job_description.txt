Senior Architect AI Engineering and Platforms
Meridian Credit Analytics LLC — Chicago, IL

Meridian Credit Analytics is building the next generation of its AI platform to serve credit, fraud and identity products. We are hiring a Senior Architect to set the technical direction for AI engineering across the company.

Responsibilities:

• Define the target architecture for enterprise AI platforms, including LLM gateways, retrieval services and model serving.

• Lead architecture reviews and publish reference architectures and engineering standards for AI workloads.

• Partner with data science, security and product teams to move models from prototype to production.

• Design scalable, secure cloud-native services on AWS and Kubernetes.

• Establish MLOps practices including model registry, CI/CD for models, and monitoring with drift detection.

• Drive responsible AI and model governance practices in a regulated environment.

• Mentor engineers and architects across multiple product teams.

Required Qualifications:

• 10+ years of software engineering experience, including 3+ years in architecture roles.

• Deep experience with AWS (EKS, SageMaker, Bedrock) and Kubernetes.

• Hands-on experience building LLM applications, including retrieval-augmented generation and prompt engineering.

• Proficiency in Python and Java.

• Experience with event-driven architecture and Apache Kafka.

• Experience with Terraform and CI/CD pipelines.

• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.

Preferred Qualifications:

• Experience with Azure OpenAI or Google Vertex AI.

• Familiarity with FCRA, GLBA or other consumer-data regulations.

• Experience with Databricks or Snowflake.

• Master's degree in a technical field.
